# Study card

One card per example. Write it to `craft/studies/<example>.md`. Name the file after the example, lower case, with dashes. A link becomes its host with dashes: example.com gives `example-com.md`.

## Fill in this card

```
# Study: <example name>
Link or file: <where it is>
Type: <website, web app, code, doc, design, app>
Studied with: <the tool you used, or "page fetch">
Date: <yyyy-mm-dd>

## What it is
<2 sentences, plain words>

## Licence and reuse
Licence: <every statement found (file, manifest, README), or "none found">
May reuse: <ideas and patterns, always>
Never reuse: <its text, art, code, name, brand, unless the licence says yes>

## Parts
| Part | What it does | How it works | Evidence | Label |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <part> | <one line> | <one line> | <what you saw> | Seen |

## Unknowns
- <thing you could not see, and how to find out>
```

## Labels

| Label | Use it when | Evidence must be |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seen | You looked at it yourself | A short quote, a file path, a line, a screen detail |
| Inferred | You worked it out from what you saw | The Seen part it comes from |
| Unknown | You could not tell | Why not |

## Rules

1. Every row has exactly one label.
2. No evidence means Unknown.
3. Need 1 or more Seen row per example.
4. A part you could not open goes in Unknowns, never in Parts as Seen.
5. Keep quotes under 15 words. Never paste the example's text or code.
6. No licence found: write "none found" and treat it as "ideas only".
7. Licence statements disagree: list them all and take ideas only.
