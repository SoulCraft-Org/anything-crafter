# Privacy

Anything Crafter is instructions in Markdown: one skill and one reviewer agent. It has no code, no hooks, no MCP servers and no connectors. SoulCraft runs no service for it.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| What does SoulCraft collect? | Nothing. The plugin sends nothing to SoulCraft, and it adds no tracking, analytics or upload of its own |
| What does it read? | Only what you point it at: the examples you name, and the files in your project |
| What does it write? | Files in your own project: the `craft/` folder and what you build. They stay on your machine or wherever your project lives |
| What does it fetch? | The public pages or repositories you name as examples, through your AI's own tools. Those sites receive each request like any web visit. The plugin adds nothing about you to it |
| What does it install? | Nothing by itself. For apps and binaries, it can suggest the open-source `rea` tool. It asks you first and never runs it without your yes |
| Who handles your chat? | Your AI provider, under its own terms. This plugin adds no other party |
| How long is data kept? | SoulCraft keeps nothing, because it receives nothing |
| Is it meant for children? | No. It is meant for people 18 and over |

Questions or a problem: open an issue or a discussion at [github.com/SoulCraft-Org/anything-crafter](https://github.com/SoulCraft-Org/anything-crafter). Security problems go through the repository's private security report, never a public issue.
