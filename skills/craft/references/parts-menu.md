# Parts menu

Write it to `craft/blend.md`. Read every file in `craft/studies/` first. No design yet.

## Fill in this menu

```
# Blend
Studies read: <E1 file, E2 file, ...>

## What I learned
- <one line a person can check against a study card>
- <one line>
- <one line>

## Parts
| # | Part | From | What it does | Label | Fits vision | Pick |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | <part> | E1 | <one line> | Seen | yes | |
| 2 | <part> | E2 | <one line> | Inferred | no: <why, for example needs a server> | |

## Overlaps and gaps
- Both do: <part both examples have>
- Only one has: <part>
- Neither has, but the brief wants: <V-line, no part yet>
```

## Steps

1. Write 3 to 6 "What I learned" lines first. State them before any design.
2. Put every part from every card in one table. One row per part.
3. Merge a part that two examples share into one row. Write both in "From".
4. Copy each part's label from its card. Never upgrade one.
5. Fill "Fits vision" with `yes`, or `no` and why when the part costs a V-line (a server, a build step). Never drop the row.
6. Leave the Pick column empty.
7. Where a part serves a V-line in the brief, add the V-number to "What it does".

## Stop 1

1. Show the whole menu in your reply, with the empty Pick column. Name each file you wrote so far.
2. Ask: "Which parts do you want? Give me numbers, or say all of the Seen ones."
3. Wait. Do not design. Do not pick or recommend parts for the person: "Fits vision" is your advice.
4. Write `yes` in the Pick column for each number given. Leave the others empty.
5. End the reply with "Skipped or unchecked: ...".

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Show part names and one-line reasons | Show code, text or art from an example |
| Name the V-line gaps no part fills | Fill a gap with a guess |
